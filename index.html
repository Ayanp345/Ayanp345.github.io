01 — Focus

My work sits at the intersection of interpretability, evaluation, and trustworthy AI — probing what's actually happening inside a model's hidden states, rather than taking its outputs on faith.

  • Mechanistic interpretability & activation probing
  • Multilingual & multimodal LLMs
  • RAG faithfulness & grounding
  • AI safety & model evaluation

I'm a final-year Computer Science & Data Analytics undergraduate at IIT Patna. At IIT Hyderabad's NLIP Lab I built a 5,000-sample culturally grounded reasoning benchmark across 100+ countries and ran activation and linear-probing experiments across five open-source LLM families. My current project asks whether retrieval-augmented financial models actually use the evidence they're given, or fall back on memorized parametric knowledge — using layer-wise probes trained on hidden states extracted before generation.
